Rural Bridge

An integrated health and agriculture risk platform for rural India. This is the build blueprint and the record of what was actually made, measured and left to do.

The competition will split into a health track and an agri track. We are building neither. We are building the join between them, because a pesticide application record and a dermal symptom report become an early warning only when something puts them in the same row.
Section one

The call

You have 11 hours and you are the only person on the prototype. That budget does not buy two full tracks, an ML model, and a polished deck. It buys one vertical slice, deployed and provably working, plus the screenshots the other four need for the deck.

So the scope is one sentence: a field worker captures a record with no signal, the record survives a force-kill, it syncs on reconnect, and a rule fires that neither feed could have produced alone. Everything else is negotiable.

Non negotiable

Offline capture

Append-only op log in IndexedDB. Survives airplane mode and a force-kill. This is the claim every judge will test.

Non negotiable

Sync drain

Idempotent POST keyed on a client-generated UUID. The queue emptying on screen is the moment that sells the whole platform.

Non negotiable

Cross-domain flag

A rule that reads both feeds and explains itself in one sentence a block officer can act on. This is the thesis.

One concern, stated once.

Deploying an offline-first PWA with a real database is genuinely achievable tonight, but only if the deploy happens in the first 30 minutes rather than the last. Teams lose this round by writing beautiful code that first meets a deployment pipeline at 22:00. The schedule below front-loads deployment for exactly that reason.

Section two

The pipeline

Two feeds enter on a shared spatial-temporal key. One ranked, explainable advisory leaves.

Capture
ASHA worker health

Voice or tap entry: symptom category, severity, patient age band. Block ID and timestamp attach automatically from the device. No signal required.

Agri worker agri

Input class, crop, application date, area covered. Same offline path, same op log, same block key.

Buffer
Append-only op log IndexedDB via Dexie

Every capture writes one immutable op with a client-generated UUIDv4, a device ID, and a monotonic sequence number. Nothing is ever edited in place, so a half-finished sync can always be resumed rather than reconciled.

Sync
Idempotent batch drain POST /api/sync

On reconnect the client posts ops in batches. The server upserts on the client UUID, so a retried batch is a no-op rather than a duplicate. Conflict policy is last-write-wins per record, which is defensible here because two workers never edit the same observation. That is the honest answer to the CRDT question, and it is a better answer than an unnecessary CRDT.

Join
Correlation engine the bridge

Rule-based, not ML. Groups both feeds by (block_id, date), computes a 60-day rolling baseline per block, and tests three rules. Rule-based is the deliberate choice: every flag can be explained to a judge, and to a district health officer, in one sentence with the numbers attached.

Enrich
Weather Open-Meteo, live, no key

Temperature and humidity per block centroid, converted to a heat index. Cached server-side so the free tier quota is never the bottleneck during a demo.

Act
Ranked advisory

Each flag carries a severity, the two source figures that produced it, and one recommended action addressed to a named role. A flag with no action attached is a statistic, and statistics do not get acted on in a block office.

Section three

Stack, and the trade made to get it

Your brief specified FastAPI and Postgres. With 11 hours and one builder I am recommending a change to the prototype only: one deployable instead of two. Two deployments means CORS, two dashboards, two sets of environment variables, and a free-tier Python host that cold-starts for 30 to 50 seconds, which is long enough to kill a live demo.

One Vercel project serving both the PWA and the API removes all of that. The API surface stays identical, so the architecture slide can still show FastAPI and Postgres as the production target as long as the deck says plainly which one is deployed. Judges reward that distinction. They punish discovering it themselves.

Section four

Data model

Agree this before any UI. Everything else is downstream of the block key.

The shared key is block_id, shaped like an LGD (Local Government Directory) code, which is the identifier both AgriStack and ABDM-adjacent state systems actually key on. Using the real scheme costs nothing now and makes the integration slide credible.

The demo geography is Yavatmal district, Maharashtra. That is a deliberate choice: Yavatmal is the site of a widely documented 2017 organophosphate poisoning cluster among cotton farmers, which makes the pesticide-to-symptom rule concrete rather than hypothetical. Have someone on the deck team pull a citable source for the figures before it goes on a slide.

Three blocks are rigged with a pesticide-to-symptom pattern, one for heat only, and one rigged to look suspicious but fall below threshold. That last one matters most: it proves the engine says no, which is the question a sharp judge asks.

Section five

The three rules

Written out with thresholds, because "our engine detects correlations" is not an answer and these numbers are what gets asked about.

Rule 1 exposure to symptom

For block B on day D, count applications of WHO Class II inputs in the window [D-7, D]. Compare against that block's median weekly count over the preceding 60 days, excluding the trailing 14 so the spike cannot inflate its own baseline.

If applications reach the greater of 3 or three times baseline, look forward across [D, D+5] at dermal, respiratory and neurological reports. Flag when those reach the greater of 4 or two and a half times their own baseline.

Rule 2 heat stress

Heat index computed from live Open-Meteo temperature and humidity at the block centroid. Two or more consecutive days at or above 40°C raises a labour advisory.

Bands: 40 to 45 caution, 45 to 52 danger, above 52 extreme. Action is a field-hours shift out of the 11:00 to 16:00 window plus an ORS distribution prompt to ASHA workers in that block.

Rule 3 the composite

A block carrying both an active heat advisory and an active exposure flag escalates one severity band, regardless of either rule's individual score.

The justification is physiological rather than statistical: heat drives sweating and higher respiratory rate, both of which increase dermal and inhalation uptake of the same compound. Neither a health system nor an agriculture system can see this. That is the entire argument for the platform, expressed as ten lines of code.

Confidence and suppression

Confidence is the observed-to-expected ratio capped at 4 and bucketed into low, medium and high. Blocks with fewer than 8 health records in the baseline window are suppressed entirely and surfaced as "insufficient data" rather than as a weak signal.

Say this part out loud in the demo. A system that admits what it cannot see reads as engineering. A system that flags everything reads as a demo.

Why rules and not a model.

There is no labelled dataset for this join, so any model would be trained on data we invented. A rule-based engine is honest about that, runs in milliseconds on a cheap phone, and survives the question "why did it flag this block" without hand-waving. Frame it as a deliberate engineering choice with a stated upgrade path, not as a limitation.

The privacy line worth rehearsing.

Section eight

What is built

Every line below was verified by running it, not by reading it. The two unverified items are named as unverified.

Done

Correlation engine and its acceptance test

Three rules with published thresholds. Flags exactly the four rigged blocks, stays silent on the deliberate near-miss and on six quiet blocks. Runs in 2 ms. npm run engine:test

Done

Schema, seed and the database round trip

Six tables. 718 rows seed in 143 ms. The seed script reads the data back out and re-runs the engine on the database's own rows to prove the round trip does not change the answer. npm run db:seed

Done

Three identical posts of the same op produced one row and three audit entries. This is the claim a judge is most likely to test, and it is testable with one curl command.

Done

Captured with the browser forced offline, confirmed present in IndexedDB, reloaded the page, confirmed it survived, then watched it auto-sync and the server row count move by exactly one.

Done

Role select, block select, field home, health capture in two steps, agri capture, outbox, officer dashboard, flag detail, consent. Hindi and Marathi across the whole field-worker path.

Done

Voice in both directions

Speech recognition in English, Hindi and Marathi maps speech onto a structured category the worker confirms. Read-aloud speaks the question back, because recognition alone helps someone who cannot type and does nothing for someone who cannot read. Real weather per block centroid, 60 past days plus a 7 day forecast, with no API key of any kind. Read-only by default: persisting it would replace the seeded scenario, and real heat here peaks outside the evaluation window, so writing sits behind an explicit flag.

Section nine

Demo script

Four minutes. Rehearse it twice tonight, out loud, on the phone you will actually hold.

0:00

Open on the flag, not the login

Start on the Ner block flag detail. Read the sentence aloud. "Nine applications against a baseline of two. Eleven dermal reports against a baseline of one point four. Heat index above 43." Then say the platform found this because it is the only system that can see both numbers.

0:45

Go backwards to the field

Switch to the ASHA role. Turn on airplane mode in front of them. Do not describe it, show the switch.

1:15

Capture with no signal

Log a dermal case in about 15 seconds. Then force-kill the app and reopen it. The record is still queued. This is the moment that separates you from every slideware entry.

2:00

Reconnect

Airplane mode off. The queue drains on screen. Point at the idempotency note without stopping.

2:30

The join closes

Back to the block officer view. The record you just captured has moved a flag. Same device, same 30 seconds, both ends of the pipeline.

3:00

Close on numbers and honesty

Bundle size, Lighthouse score, cold load on throttled network. Then name what is simulated and why, in one sentence, before anyone asks. Finish on the QR code and invite them to open it on their own phone.

Have these three answers ready.
  • Why not machine learning. No labelled dataset exists for this join, so a model would be trained on data we invented. Rules are explainable to a district officer and they are auditable. The upgrade path is real data from a pilot.
  • What happens when two devices conflict. They do not. Each observation is written once by one worker and is immutable, so last-write-wins on the client UUID is correct rather than convenient. A CRDT here would be complexity without a conflict to resolve.
  • Is this AgriStack data. No. It is a mock adapter with the correct schema shape, because third-party AgriStack API access is not open to us. Say that plainly and show the adapter interface. Getting caught overstating this is fatal, and admitting it costs nothing.
